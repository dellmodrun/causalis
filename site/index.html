The state atlasfour regimes of agency

Autonomy is not binary.

A system can hold keys without forming intentions, execute instructions without learning, or learn without permission to act. The atlas separates these regimes so that the word “agent” does not conceal the mechanism.

low agency bounded agency adaptive agency
R₀

Passive capital

Assets remain inert until an external signer supplies an action.

observe 0 · decide 0 · execute 0
R₁

Automated capital

Fixed rules execute repeatedly, but the policy does not update itself.

observe 1 · decide 0 · execute 1
R₂

Bounded agency

A policy selects among permitted actions inside an explicit mandate.

observe 1 · decide 1 · execute 1
R₃

Adaptive agency

State, policy and reachable actions co-evolve with the environment.

observe 1 · learn 1 · intervene 1

The observatorysix surfaces of measurement
